WHO I AM

Bridging low-level hardware constraints with high-level intelligence and real-time physical telemetry.

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Marquette University (B.S. May 2026) with deep engineering focus across embedded systems, robotics, edge sensing, and on-device machine learning.

Most recently I served as a Research Fellow at Marquette's Opus College of Engineering, implementing an Exponentially Weighted Recursive Least Squares Estimation (EWRLSE) algorithm in embedded C and Python for real-time halogenated hydrocarbon detection via Surface Acoustic Wave (SAW) sensors.

On the hardware bench, I design in KiCad and Altium, interface mixed I2C/SPI/UART buses, and validate with oscilloscopes and logic analyzers.

// ATMOSPHERIC ROBOTICS & SENSING

Autonomous Meteorological UAV System

An autonomous, long-endurance meteorological unmanned aerial system engineered for atmospheric boundary layer profiling and micro-climate observation. Designed to bridge the gap between fixed weather stations and expensive radiosonde weather balloons.

Features a custom-designed sensor payload bay housing high-precision barometric pressure (BMP390, ±3 Pa), digital temperature/relative humidity (SHT45), a 2D ultrasonic anemometer for real-time wind vector extraction, and optical particulate sensors.

The avionics stack runs custom FreeRTOS firmware on an STM32F4, executing an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) for altitude hold, synchronized DMA sensor sampling at 20 Hz, and telemetry packet serialization over a long-range 915 MHz LoRa transceiver to a portable ground station with real-time thermodynamic Skew-T plotting.

// QUANTITATIVE ML & RISK PRICING

Weather Derivatives Machine Learning Modeling

Developing predictive machine learning architectures to model non-linear meteorological patterns, forecast temperature/precipitation volatility, and price financial weather derivatives (CME Heating Degree Days [HDD] and Cooling Degree Days [CDD] index options).

Ingests over 30 years of daily atmospheric surface records from NOAA GHCN-Daily and ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis, extracting cyclic seasonal harmonics, teleconnection indices (North Atlantic Oscillation, ENSO), and local thermodynamic drift.

Couples PyTorch LSTM recurrent networks and XGBoost ensembles with mean-reverting Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic processes. Executes 100,000-path Monte Carlo simulations to accurately price seasonal volumetric risk contracts and optimize hedging portfolios for agricultural and energy utility markets.
